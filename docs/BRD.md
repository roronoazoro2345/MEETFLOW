Project Name: MeetFlowDocument Version: 1.0Status: Draft / Proposed1. Executive Summary1.1 Project OverviewMeetFlow is a centralized, web-based SaaS platform designed to streamline the entire lifecycle of organizational meetings—from scheduling and live execution to post-meeting action item tracking and analytics. By offering native third-party video platform integrations, standardized Minutes of Meeting (MoM) templates, granular task management, and automated reporting, MeetFlow transforms meetings from time-consuming events into actionable business outcomes.1.2 Business VisionTo provide a unified workspace that eliminates fragmented communication tools, driving accountability, transparency, and measurable team productivity across organizations.2. Business Problem & Project Objectives2.1 Problem StatementModern organizations face operational inefficiencies due to tool fragmentation:Disjointed Workflows: Teams use separate tools for video calls (Google Meet/MS Teams), task tracking (Excel), documentation (Word), and communication (Email/WhatsApp).Operational Friction: Results in misplaced meeting notes, forgotten action items, poor cross-departmental visibility, lack of task accountability, and zero capability to measure meeting effectiveness.2.2 Project ObjectivesCentralize Workflows: Combine scheduling, meeting link generation, documentation, task assignment, and performance reporting into a single platform.Automate Integration: Enable one-click link generation for Google Meet and Microsoft Teams via direct API calls.Drive Accountability: Link action items directly to meeting contexts with real-time tracking, due dates, and status updates.Quantify Productivity: Offer analytics on team performance, task completion trends, and meeting effectiveness.3. Stakeholders & User RolesRoleKey Responsibilities & PermissionsAdminOrganization-wide administration, managing users and departments, role assignments, global configuration of integrations, full audit log access, and high-level analytical reporting.ManagerScheduling and managing meetings, generating meeting links, facilitating discussions, recording MoMs, assigning action items, tracking team progress, and viewing performance reports.EmployeeViewing scheduled meetings, joining calls, reviewing MoMs, managing assigned tasks (updating progress, adding comments/attachments), and receiving real-time notifications.4. sent to participants.Module 4: Notifications EngineIn-App Alerts: Real-time updates delivered via SignalR for meeting changes, task assignments, due dates, and completion status.Email Notifications:
